### Description
Upon a hit against a limb, tattoos cover it. Each tattoo is a reoccurring pattern of shark bites.

- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Components:** Somatic (x)
- **Action Cost:** Bonus Action
- **Length:** 1
- **Effect:** That limb is now paralyzed and shreaking with the pain of being bitten by a shark. 1d10 psychic damage
