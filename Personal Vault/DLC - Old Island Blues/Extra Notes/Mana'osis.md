The disease/cancer/virus/whatever it is that causes archetypes

### Theme: Growth 

#### Origins:
Proteans first came to the Hawaiian islands long before humans every arrived. They were turned away by the land due to the lack of large animals. These Proteans were all of leviathan or behemoth class, making their diets big asf(tubby). They mostly stuck the sea and deep ocean where they could feed on larger creatures. 

Eventually, the Polynesians would begin to sail the pacific and occasionally a large Protean would spot their canoes and attack, consuming them all. These attacks where rare and not always pleasant for the Protean as many wood chips and splinters would cover their mouth. 

Some Proteans would begin to watch the humans though, and see their strange practices. Most important was growing and hunting food, along with their keen reliance on religion. Some of the Proteans would get the bright idea of transforming themselves to powerful human forms and gaining the worship of humans. They would receive gifts of food and/or sacrifice from the humans and in return, they gave the humans something to worship along with occasionally helping them. This is how the Polynesian people got their gods and deities.

Eventually this connection between the races would have unexpected effects as the Proteans passed on Mana'osis or as the WHITE man calls it, Manosis. This powerful disease would spread all throughout the Hawaiian people, manifesting as their belief in Mana, ones spiritual and physical power. Mana'osis began to change the most powerful of the Hawaiian people, and with activation from Photogen storms or powerful Protean "Deities," archetypes would manifest in the Hawaiian people, creating stories of powerful Ali'i rulers and Maui. 
#### How it works:
Mana'osis is all about growth. It attaches itself to the muscles, brain, and much of the body of the infected and grows with them. The stronger, smarter, more confidence, and overall more powerful the person, the exponentially more concentrated and powerful the Mana'osis. Higher amounts of Mana'osis allow for a higher chance that it can be activated into an archetype, along with amounts equal more powerful person as the disease improves you.

If someone with Mana'osis slays someone else infected, Mana'osis has a strange trait of passing itself through any medium possible to the slayer. The Mana'osis will adapt to the new body and empower it, making the slayer more powerful.

An archetype can be awakened in someone with high amounts of Mana'osis by a special protean activating it for you or you get lucky while passing through a protogen storm. 

#### Mana'osis Artifacts:
If Mana'osis infected blood is spilled on an object, there's a chance that over a Mana'osis archetype user's lifetime, enough of their cells and the Mana'osis cells will be left on the object that the Mana'osis cells are kept alive and thriving on the object. They will begin to infest that object. This will weave the Mana'osis and cells of the archetype user into the object. Upon the users death, a small portion of their Mana'osis cells will pass onto the object, giving it a small part of their archetype. This new Mana'osis empowered item can have a powerful ability that any user can use for their own goals
