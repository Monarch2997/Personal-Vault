# **Resuscitation Edict:**
## Demigod
**Description**
Your mortal wounds begin to emit waves of mana into the surrounding spaces. You are given a second chance at life. You can either kill someone to replenish the lost mana and gain the benefit, you can absord an object with high mana or spiritual power, or you can put yourself in the debt of a god(promising to pay back reality itself). Once done, mana swaps from emitting to drawing in. A great implosion of mana surround the archetype user and fills their wounds.
**Activation**
You either kill someone, absorb an item, or pledge yourself to a god(reality). Pledging yourself is done by just screaming outward into the sky or land
**Removals**
all negative effects, and weaknesses for 3 turns
**Effect**
All health is regained, negatives effects are removed, and you are empowered. Essentially you get a long rest in an instant. Along with that, for three turns you are in a demigod state, meaning no weaknesses, 20 in every stat, and resistance to all types of damage

If you pledged yourself to a god, roll the table for gods and they will each have a consequence

# **Incipent Ability:**

## Ability: Breath of Life

### Description
Breath life into the land and yourself. Your chest glows bright with a warm glow, breathing out rays of sunlight, you bring life to the land in front of you within a 30ft cone. The more dead land you breath life into, the more health you gain.
### Effect

- **Cooldown:** 0
- **Components:** Verbal, Somatic (breathing)
- **Action Cost:** Bonus action
- **Length:** instant
- **Effect:** regain 1d4 hp for each 5ft by 5ft square healed
