# **Resuscitation Edict:**
## Ravage & Rebuild
**Description**
All current Menehune go into a frenzy, red eyes and frantic movements. They will run around and attack anything human, ripping and tearing body parts and flesh from them. If they get a piece, they will run back to the [[Archetyper]] to repair the wound. Once they have taken enough to repair the injuries the [[Archetyper]] has incurred, they return to life fully healed and rested.
**Activation**
Death
**Removals**
All status effects besides diseases
**Effect**
Replace all Menehune with frenzied Menehune. If there are no Menehune, spawn 5 more. Each of the Menehune will charge towards a human body and attempt to rip parts out to take back. Smaller wounds will be a roll to hit and a strength check while larger limbs will be a few turns and take many strength checks to rip off. Once a body part is taken back, the Menehune will repair that part of the body.
# **Incipent Ability:**
## Harvest

### Description
You have been blessed with 5 menehune. You can send them out to harvest construction supplies from the environment(nuts, bolts, scrap, trash, etc).
### Effect

- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Components:** verbal(call the menehune to you)
- **Action Cost:** Action, Bonus Action
- **Length:** forever
- **Effect:**
 Upon use of this ability(bonus action), summon up to 5 Menehune, each anywhere within a 10ft radius. You can send them to any sort of supply in your environment and they will harvest it. Then you can have them move it anywhere you'd like. 

Each Menehune has a storage of one supply, you have a limitless storage

As an action, you can have your Menehune craft an item, they can craft it by coming together with supplies from other Menehune, craft it on their own if they have the supplies, or around you as you are the supply carrier(You can store much more supplies than the Menehune can in their hands). 

Menehune have their own turns but they can only move or collect an item. Crafting is an order by you

Menehune also follow their mythological rules, so they can be temporarily pacified by bananas or fish like a dog and steak.
