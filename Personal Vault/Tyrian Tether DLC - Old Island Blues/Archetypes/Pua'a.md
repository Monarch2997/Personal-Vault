# **Resuscitation Edict:** Corpse Eater
## Edict: 
**Description**
Your cells form together to fire out hundreds of tusks in all directions. Every body hit, when they become a corpse, you can choose to use their cells to rebuild yourself or turn them into a basic boar monster. These tusks can last eternally
**Activation**
Death
**Removals**
You can't remove the tusks without a complete body swap
**Effect**
If you are hit (dex save), you may turn into a boar or tusk on death. If you have your own resuscitation effect, you must make an saving throw using your best stat (DC [[Archetyper]] level + 10).
