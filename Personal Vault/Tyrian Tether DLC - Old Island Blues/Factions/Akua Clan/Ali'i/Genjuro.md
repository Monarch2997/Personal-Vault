Japanese

Born Osaka, Japan

Leader of the Engineers

**Backstory:**

Genjuro has lived a simple life. He grew up to a very standard japanese family in Osaka who pushed him very hard in school. He would go on to be accepted into the University of Tokyo majoring in Engineering. He would diligently spend two years there, but college life would change him. He grew up with a great love for action manga, especially those where the characters had powers. He would be inspired by their wild stories to try and make some of his own while away from his family. This would lead to him getting big into the Tokyo drag racing scene. Nearly every night he would be out with his friends speeding and doing tricks in their sport cars.  This would begin to tank Genjuro's grades and he was facing expulsion due to failing so many classes. He was talented though and would use his engineering skills to create a specialized nitro device that ended up sending his car flying ahead of the others in a race. The speed was an insane 280 mph. Although the engineering feat was insane, he would be unable to stop the car as it crashed through a few empty university classrooms. He would immediately be expelled and placed in jail awaiting trial. 

This stunt would get the attention of the [[Akua Clan]]. Desperate for people talented with technology and in a bad spot, they reached out to him and offered him bail, a job, and a much cooler life. Genjuro was desperate and took this offer immediately, worried about going back to his parents. Genjuro would then be smuggled out of Japan and brought to the clan's secret research base near Lua Kealialuna. There he spends every week working as their top protean research engineer to develop their Spirit Tec. In return, he gets a great salary and every weekend gets to go do races and more in Honolulu. 

He is the least loyal of the leaders of the [[Akua Clan ]]. He is kept away from the players so he wont be discovered for some time. Although he is talented, he doesn't care much for the clans true goals and is just helping to escape the law and make great money.
