#### New Lore 

The overall lore mission of Old Island Blues will be to delve more into the diversity of Archetypes and protean influences on the world. Archetypes will be extremely prevalent as most fights will involve an archetype user. Far less general soldiers would exist for the main villains. Around 10 archetypes, including players and main villains, are planned to be showcased in the DLC. Many of the new archetypes will be more environmentally focused and less time and reality focused as seen with Janus and Adeline. As far as Proteans, they will have a deep connection to the nation of Kanakai'na and the players will discover their long standing influence on the people there. While other expansions or games may cover the actual beginnings of the Proteans, Old Island Blues will showcase more of their unique forms and history on Elpis.

#### Themes:

The nation of Kamakai'na has suffered deeply due to major global influences and colonization, mostly due to Nova Rheon and the Caelum Dominion. Due to this, there is a lot of inequality and long standing effects of colonialism, even though now in 1599, the nation is independent. Colonization has left a brutal mark on the Kai'nan people though, and this story explore two approaches to overcoming colonialism. The main villain shows harsh conservatism and fighting back through violence and sacrifice. Their methods would put Kamakai'na back into the past and force it to stay the same. They dream of the old ways, and will do whatever it takes to go back to what they see as their true home. Imua and Kona each show their own approach. Kona approaches the challenges with using traditional values to hold strong against the tide of hardship and to slowly adapt to the changed world. Imua believes that the Kai'nan people have been forced into the colonizers ways and so he must massively succeed at the colonizer's games in order to gain the power to use their system against them and free the Kai'nan people from the chains and scars of colonialism.
