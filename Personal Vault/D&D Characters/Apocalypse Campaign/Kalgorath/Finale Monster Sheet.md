***Matter is my  Own.*** Kalgorath has a certain level of matter that has carried with them into this form. His body flows and reshapes as needed. His matter starts at 100%. He has several actions he can take that will reshape his matter to suit his needs. Different abilities and attacks may cost matter. If Kalgorath is ever to reach 10% matter, he will gain the frightened condition to everyone and his only goal will be to reach a secluded space to warp away to Tarcas. If Kalgorath is ever to reach 0% matter, he immediately warped back to Tarcas and the location of Tarcas is permanently known to all.
### Actions
***Form Melee.*** Select a Weapon and make the respective weapon attacks. A melee attack will result in -5% matter. All weapons have reach as a base:

**Old-stone:** Smash them with rock, make one attack at 2d12 bludgeoning damage

**Pikes-of-plenty:** Sharp metal spikes spur fourth from Kalgorath, make 4 attacks at 1d6 piercing damage

**Mountainous-Scraper:** Rough terrain ripped across skin, Make 6 attacks at 1d4 slashing damage

*Then, select a clever trick, or not (-3% matter):*

**Clacketing stones within:** Add 1d10 thunder damage 

**Citric coat:** Add 2d4+2 acid damage

**Nitrogen vent:** Add 3d4-2 cold damage

**Molten-Core:** Add 1d8+2 fire damage

***Cast Occult.*** Spells are just ranged extensions of Kalgorath's matter. Be careful to get your matter back. Roll an intelligence saving throw. On a fail, lose 10% matter. On a success, lose 5% matter. Spells can continue to exist past your initial turn but every turn they exist for and affect a target, Kalgorath loses 5% matter per spell kept going.

###### Spellcasting Modifier: +11 Spell Save DC: 19

**Tornado of Souls:** A ripping windstorm flows into the field as Kalgorath himself transforms. Hands of the damned reach out for salvation. 15ft radius tornado appears. It can move up to 15ft per turn. On contact with a target, they must make a dex save or suffer 5d10 force damage and 1d10 psychic damage. 4d10 force damage on success. Kalgorath has an ac of 18 while in this state but cannot make other moves.

**Death & Decay:** The land itself is broken down into raw minerals. In a 15ft cube, deployed within 60ft, all matter must make a wisdom saving throw or suffer 3d12 necrotic damage. Reduce constitution by -2 until end of next turn. Half on success and no effect.

**Surging flame geysers:** A fireball tossed, a geyser sprouts, and the ball jumps to the next spot. Select 3 spots within 45 feet. Each spot must be generally in a straight or slightly curved line from the last. No more than 15 feet in distance from the last. Each spot, in a 10ft radius, all creature must make a dexterity saving throw or suffer 8d6 fire damage. Half on success

**Atmosphere of Divine origin:** A crushing pressure and noxious air fills the room Everyone, except Kalgorath, in a 15ft radius must make a constitution saving throw or suffers -2 to all physical ability score checks and saving throws. -1 on success. 

***Harvest Local Matter.*** 

Outside of combat, this ability can be used to bring matter back to 90%, if battle was a total success. After each battle survived, lower that max by 10% each. (ex. after the next its 80%)

Inside of combat: Any active spell can be cancelled to regain 5% matter. Regain 5d2% matter as a base
### Bonus Actions
***Reshape Body.*** Condense or expand your size to a new form. +10% matter to go down a tier, -10% to go up a tier:

**Small** (STR: 6, DEX: 14, CON: 6, +4 to spellcasting modifier, Speed: 60ft, AC: 14)

**Medium** (STR: 10, DEX: 10, CON: 10, +0 to spellcasting modifier, Speed: 30ft, AC: 10)

**Large** (STR: 14, DEX: 6, CON: 14, -4 to spellcasting modifier, Speed: 15ft, AC: 14)

***Reform wound.*** Heal any amount of damage taken. Take the total amount of health regained, divide it in half (Rounded up), and subtract that as a percentage from total matter
### Reactions
***Disperse Form.*** Set AC to 25 before you know the roll of that attack. You cannot take actions in this state. You may only take reshape body as a bonus action. You may only pick medium.
