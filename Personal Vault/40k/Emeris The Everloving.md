#### Overview
1. born on Barbarous. Mother served in the Death Guard. Father out of the picture. Mother died in a necrolyte death gas attack. They had two gas masks. His stopped working so his mother sacrificed herself to give him hers. He tried to save her with their medical supplies but he didn't really understand any and hurt more than he helped. She then told him that her fellow soldiers in the Death Guard could care for him. Although she showed no fear and no negative emotion towards Emeris, he would still blame himself for hurting her and being unable to heal her in her death.
2. He would eventually become a space marine for the Death Guard, allowing him to fight in the Great Crusade. This training would be horrible for Emeris. Barbarous raised tough and resilient warriors for the Death Guard. Emeris failed to meet these expectations. He struggled to grow out of his childish nature and the loss he experienced left him deeply sensitive.
